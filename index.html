A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Cover letter generator FAQ

Is this AI cover letter generator free?

Yes. You can use the generator on this page without creating an account. AI generation requires the service to be available.

What should I paste into the resume field?

Paste your resume text or a concise list of relevant experience, skills, and achievements. Avoid including information you do not want processed by the AI service.

Will the generator invent experience for me?

The generator is instructed to use only the details you provide. Always review the draft and confirm every claim before using it.

What happens to my resume and job description?

Your text is sent to Google Gemini to produce the cover letter. This app does not save the submitted text or generated draft.
